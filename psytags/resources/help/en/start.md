# Please read first

## Test on copies first

| PsyTags writes directly to your music files and renames them. So copy a few albums into a folder of their own and try everything there, before you settle on your workflow. |
|---|

Only move on to your real collection once the result looks the way you want it.

A backup of your collection is a good idea in any case.

## What PsyTags changes

- **Tags:** title, album, year, numbers, genres, moods and covers are written to the files.
- **File and folder names:** only change when you run **Rename** (Alt+1).
- **Deleting:** files are never gone for good. They go to the recycle bin, and only when you confirmed it or set it up that way.

The Del key only removes files from the list. They stay on your disk.

## When something goes wrong

**Undo** (Ctrl+Z) takes back changes to tags and renames, even a whole automatic run, album by album. This only works while the program is running. Anything in the recycle bin can be restored from there.
